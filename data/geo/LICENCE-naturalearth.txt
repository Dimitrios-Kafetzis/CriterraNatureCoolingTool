Note on this redistribution
---------------------------

Attribution is not required by these terms. It is given anyway, in NOTICE and
in data/geo/ATTRIBUTION.md, because a reader of an assessment is entitled to
know the provenance of a boundary the tool used to identify their country.

The bundled file is not the publisher's original. Its derivation — which
attributes were kept, what coordinate precision was retained, and which three
entries were omitted for want of an ISO code — is recorded in
data/geo/ATTRIBUTION.md and performed by tools/build_datasets.py.
